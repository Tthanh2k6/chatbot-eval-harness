# Quillnote Pricing Plans

Quillnote offers three subscription tiers: Free, Plus, and Team. The Free plan costs $0 per month and includes up to 250 notes, 50 MB of monthly upload quota, and syncing across 2 devices. The Plus plan costs $8 per month (or $72 per year when billed annually) and unlocks unlimited notes, 10 GB of monthly storage uploads, and syncing across unlimited devices.

The Quillnote Team plan costs $15 per user per month with a minimum seat requirement of 3 seats (or $144 per user per year when billed annually). Team plan accounts receive a shared collaborative workspace, centralized administrator billing, 50 GB of monthly upload quota per seat, and priority email support with a 4-hour response SLA.

Verified students and educators qualify for a 50% discount on the annual Plus plan, reducing the subscription price to $36 per year upon academic email verification. Every new Quillnote account can start a 14-day free trial of the Plus or Team plan without entering a credit card, and workspaces automatically downgrade to the Free tier if no payment method is added before the trial ends.
