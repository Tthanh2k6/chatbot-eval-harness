# Quillnote Account Deletion

Account owners can permanently delete their Quillnote account by navigating to Settings > Account > Delete Account and typing the confirmation phrase `DELETE MY NOTES`. For security verification, you must also re-enter your account password or confirm a 6-digit authentication code if two-factor authentication is enabled on your profile.

After requesting account deletion, the account enters a 14-day deactivation grace period before permanent erasure takes place. During this 14-day grace period, you can cancel the scheduled deletion and restore full access to your notes by signing back in and selecting "Cancel Deletion". Any active paid subscription is canceled immediately upon submitting the deletion request.

Once the 14-day deactivation grace period expires, all notes, notebooks, tags, and uploaded file attachments are permanently purged from production databases within 72 hours. For Team plan workspaces, individual member seats must be removed by an administrator first, and a Team workspace can only be deleted after all secondary seats have been removed.
