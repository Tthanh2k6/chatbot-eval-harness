# Quillnote Data Retention

Deleted notes in Quillnote are moved to the Trash folder rather than erased immediately. On the Free plan, deleted notes in the Trash folder are retained for 30 days before automatic permanent deletion. On the Plus and Team plans, Trash retention is extended to 90 days so users have extra time to recover accidentally deleted notes.

While you are editing a note, Quillnote saves automatic version history snapshots every 10 minutes. Free plan accounts retain 7 days of version history snapshots, Plus plan accounts retain 180 days of version history, and Team plan accounts retain 365 days of version history with one-click snapshot restoration.

Production database backups are encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption and protected in transit with TLS 1.3, with encrypted off-site backups rotating on a 35-day retention schedule. Server diagnostic logs and client IP addresses are retained for 90 days for security auditing and abuse prevention before being permanently anonymized.
