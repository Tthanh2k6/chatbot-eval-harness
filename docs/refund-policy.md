# Quillnote Refund Policy

Quillnote offers a 30-day money-back guarantee on all first-time annual Plus and Team subscriptions. For monthly Plus or Team subscriptions, refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of the initial billing charge. Automatic subscription renewal charges are eligible for a full refund only if requested within 48 hours of the renewal date.

To request a refund, email `billing@quillnote.example.com` or submit a billing request through Settings > Billing > Request Refund with your invoice number (beginning with `INV-`). Approved refunds are processed back to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days, and the workspace immediately downgrades to the Free plan.

Accounts terminated for violating the Quillnote Acceptable Use Policy are not eligible for any refund. In addition, subscription payments made using prepaid gift cards or cryptocurrency cannot be refunded to the original payment instrument and are instead issued as non-expiring Quillnote account credit.
