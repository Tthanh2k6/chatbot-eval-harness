# Quillnote Sharing and Collaboration

Notes and notebooks in Quillnote can be shared via private email invitations or public read-only web links. Collaboration permissions support three access levels: Viewer (read-only), Commenter (can read and leave inline comments), and Editor (can modify text and attachments). Public share links can be protected with an optional password and a custom expiration date between 1 day and 90 days.

Collaborator limits depend on your subscription tier: on the Free plan, a user can invite up to 3 collaborators per note. The Plus plan allows up to 25 collaborators per note or notebook, while the Team plan supports up to 200 internal or external collaborators per shared notebook. Real-time simultaneous editing supports up to 15 active cursors in a single note at once.

Note owners can revoke a share link or remove a collaborator at any time from the Share menu, which terminates active sessions within 60 seconds. Team plan administrators can also disable external public share links organization-wide and view a 180-day sharing audit log showing who accessed or downloaded shared notes.
